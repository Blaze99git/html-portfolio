---
id: OPS-004
title: Compressed air pressure alarm response
category: maintenance
section: Utilities alarm
revision: 1.0
---
A low compressed-air pressure alarm can affect clamp and actuator behavior. Pause the affected station using the normal station control and notify the shift lead. Check the local gauge only if the area is safe and the operator is trained; do not adjust regulators or disconnect air lines. If the alarm remains active, record the pressure reading, station identifier, and alarm time, then call maintenance. Resume production only after the authorized person confirms stable pressure and the station passes its recovery check.
