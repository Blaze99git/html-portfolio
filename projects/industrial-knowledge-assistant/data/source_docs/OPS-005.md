---
id: OPS-005
title: Equipment lubrication inspection
category: maintenance
section: Planned maintenance
revision: 1.0
---
Perform lubrication inspections at the interval listed on the approved maintenance schedule. Before inspection, follow the site energy isolation procedure if access requires reaching into a guarded area. Use only the lubricant and amount specified by the equipment owner. Record the inspection date, equipment identifier, finding, and completion status in the maintenance record. Escalate leakage, unusual noise, heat, or damaged fittings instead of treating them as routine lubrication.
