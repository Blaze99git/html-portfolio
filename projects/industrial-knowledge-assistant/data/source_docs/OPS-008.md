---
id: OPS-008
title: Unit traceability and final test
category: quality
section: End-of-line record
revision: 1.0
---
At final test, verify that the unit trace identifier is readable and associated with the active work order before recording the result. Save the test status, completion time, station identifier, and required measurement set. If a scan is missing or the work-order association does not match, stop the normal completion transaction and ask the shift lead to resolve the record. A successful test result without a trace association is not a complete record.
