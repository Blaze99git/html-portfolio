---
id: OPS-001
title: Shift start and handover checklist
category: operations
section: Starting a shift
revision: 1.0
---
At shift start, review the outgoing operator handover notes and open work orders. Confirm that the line status board matches the work area, check that required guards are in place, and verify the emergency stop circuit is reset by an authorized operator. Record the shift start time and any unresolved issue in the handover log. Do not restart a stopped station until its hold reason has been reviewed and cleared.
