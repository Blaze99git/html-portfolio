---
id: OPS-003
title: Quality hold and material segregation
category: quality
section: Nonconforming material
revision: 1.0
---
If a unit does not meet an inspection requirement, place it on quality hold and identify it using the approved hold label. Keep the unit in the designated review area so it cannot re-enter normal flow. Record the work order, unit serial or trace identifier, inspection result, and time. Only an authorized quality representative can release or disposition a held unit.
