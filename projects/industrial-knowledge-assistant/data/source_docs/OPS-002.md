---
id: OPS-002
title: Recording a station stop
category: operations
section: Downtime recording
revision: 1.0
---
When a station stops unexpectedly, record the event time, station identifier, work order, and observable symptom in the downtime log. Select the closest approved downtime category, then add a short factual note. Keep observation separate from suspected cause. Escalate a repeated stop to the shift lead and leave the event open until the station has been checked and the recovery is recorded.
